Games

Cost, prizes and odds for each mini-game. Manager only.

Chances are percentages and must add up to 100 before you can save. Expected payout is the average credit paid per play. RTP compares that to what the points cost players to earn, using your deposit rate (—). Under 100% means the house is ahead. Note points are also given away free by check-in, tasks and promos, so the real return to a player is higher than the number shown.

Withdraw rules

Free credit withdrawal bands. Manager only.

Bands are read smallest first. A band covers everything up to and including its Up to amount. Hit is the free credit multiplied by the wagering figure. The largest band catches everything above it, so no amount is ever left without a rule. Set wagering to 0 for no requirement.

Changing these only affects free credit approved from now on. Credits already given keep the terms the player was promised.

VIP bonuses

Weekly bonus + tier-upgrade bonus for each rank. Manager only.

Weekly bonus is what a player at that rank can claim once per week. Tier upgrade bonus is a one-time bonus when a player first reaches that rank in a month. Rank names and the deposit needed to reach each rank are fixed.

Changing these only affects bonuses given from now on. A weekly bonus already claimed, or an upgrade bonus already granted this month, keeps its old amount.

🤝 Referral program

Staff link friends in Players → Referral (or “Referred by” when creating a player). When the friend has a deposit keyed in under Manage → Deposits, the player who invited them gets PTS automatically — even if the friend deposited before being linked. Deposit requests don’t count. 0 = off.
